# AI Usage Log

This document records the relevant use of Artificial Intelligence during the project.

## Purpose

The objective of this document is to provide traceability and transparency regarding the use of AI tools throughout the software development process.

## Principles

- AI can propose solutions, but the team makes all final decisions.
- All generated content must be reviewed and understood.
- AI suggestions must be validated before integration.
- No AI-generated code will be accepted without human supervision.
- The development team remains responsible for all delivered work.
